🧭 Outcome Areas, Leads and Targets

🌾 Food Security

Strengthened food systems and agriculture productivity.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: WFP, FAO
  • Support to 2.5–3 million people with integrated food security assistance in IPC Phase 4+ areas.
  • A 10% IPC Phase 3+ reduction target translating to approximately 770,000 people needing to shift to IPC Phase 2 or better.
  • Provision of climate-smart agriculture (CSA) inputs to 100,000 smallholder farmers.
  • Expansion of climate-resilient cultivation across 10,000 hectares of farmland.
  • Contribution to 20,000–30,000 metric tons of locally produced food in targeted counties.

🏥 Basic / Essential Social Services

Sustained access to health, education, skills development and nutrition services.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: WHO, UNICEF, NGO Forum
  • 3.2 million people in need access essential health including HIV, TB & Malaria services.
  • 200,000 out-of-school children and adolescents (50% girls) enrolled in formal/non-formal education.
  • 150,000 crisis-affected children (50% girls) supported with temporary learning spaces, materials, and psychosocial support.
  • 600,000 lower primary children reached with foundational literacy/numeracy interventions.
  • 30,000 adolescents benefit from life skills, vocational, and digital skills programmes
  • 1.2 million children with severe and moderate acute malnutrition (SAM/MAM) receive treatment.
  • 816 targeted health facilities deliver the full Essential Health Services Package.
  • Public health emergencies rapidly detected and responded within 48h-72hrs.
  • 50% Antenatal care (first visit) coverage nationally.

🏘 Durable Solutions

Reduced number of people in prolonged displacement, including refugees.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: UNDP, IOM, UNHCR, NGO Forum
  • 60,000 displaced persons receive targeted assistance to support their sustainable return, local integration or relocation (disaggregated by type of support).
  • Closure of 1 camp in Wau.
  • 75% of displacement affected people in potential areas of solutions that have access to basic services. (disaggregated by host community, refugees, IDPs and returnees).
  • 5 government led area based durable solutions roadmaps/action plans developed/updated with meaningful input from relevant communities.
  • At least 3 state-level durable solutions coordination mechanisms operationalized.

⚡ Rapid Response

Life-saving assistance and protection following new shocks.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: UNHCR, NRC
  • 65 % of people in need following a new shock reached with first-line life-saving protection and assistance. (floods, disease outbreak, conflict)
  • 40% of the annual new shock-affected caseload is reached within 14 days.
  • Protection mainstreamed and embedded in 100% of response .
  • Early warning & disaster preparedness mechanisms strengthened for improved coordination and assessment capacities.

🕊 Peace, Governance and Justice

Community violence reduction, civic engagement, rule of law, access to justice and human rights.
→ Click to explore details
Lead: UNDP, UNMISS
  • At least 12 community peace committees or platforms operational and engaging in conflict mitigation, with 30% youth and 40% women representation.
  • 30,000 people reached with civic education on constitution-making, elections, and transitional justice, disaggregated by sex and age.
  • Mobile access to justice deployed in at least 8 conflict affected locations.
  • 300 justice sector actors have enhanced skills and capacities for investigations, prosecutions, adjudication and other related skills.
  • 3,000-4,000 individuals receive legal aid (through mobile clinics + paralegal partners).
  • 600 duty bearers have enhanced SGBV (focus on targeted training in 5–6 counties).
